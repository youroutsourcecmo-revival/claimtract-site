ClaimTract Keep-Alive Service

Ezekiel Enterprises LLC — ClaimTract

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

ClaimTract Keep-Alive is an optional, separate subscription available to eligible customers who discontinue active ClaimTract Pro services but wish to preserve their existing ClaimTract website.

Cancelling ClaimTract Pro never removes previously approved articles. They stay published on ClaimTract's domain with their original attribution. Keep-Alive keeps your contact panel on them, so readers who want a public insurance adjuster can still reach you.

While Keep-Alive is active, your existing articles keep your contact panel, its supported contact pathways to your business telephone number or other designated contact destination, and supported internal links, subject to technical availability.

Keep-Alive is a static service. It does not include new articles, new content generation, ongoing publishing, content research, content updates, or active optimization.

The customer receives a limited license to keep the contact panel displayed on the existing articles for as long as the Keep-Alive subscription remains active and in good standing.

The customer does not acquire ownership of the website, articles, content clusters, internal linking architecture, or other ClaimTract-created materials.

Pricing and billing terms will be presented separately before enrollment.

If Keep-Alive is cancelled or payment obligations are not satisfied, the articles remain published on ClaimTract's domain with their original attribution, the contact panel and contact pathways are removed or replaced, and ClaimTract will not present the customer as a current subscriber, as described in Section 6.8 of the Terms of Use.
