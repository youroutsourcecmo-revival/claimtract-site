Why this is free

You shouldn't have to beg for somebody else's business.

In my years as a public adjuster, I saw firsthand that we are the most overlooked people in the claim, looked down on by insurance companies and left out when referrals get handed out. Nobody sends you leads. The only thing we got to eat was what we caught, but a page with your own name on it gives homeowners a place to find and verify you without you begging anyone for work. Wouldn't it feel better to stand on your own name than to wait for someone else to hand you a phone number?

When you don't have a system that brings leads to you, a system that makes your phone ring, you end up depending on roofers, plumbers and HVAC contractors for scraps, and the person handing over the scrap usually wants to tell you how to run the claim. A roofing contractor once gave me a few leads and then told me I worked for him, but I told him to keep his leads. Have you ever had to bite your tongue just to keep a referral source happy?

Most adjusters get one half-baked lead every few months, and when it's the only lead you've got, you end up bending over backwards for it. My goal is the opposite: adjusters with enough homeowners finding them that they can pick the claims they want, turn down the ones they don't, and walk in with their chest out. A page of your own is where that starts. Wouldn't it feel good to be the one choosing?

The catch? There isn't one. No price, no countdown. What do you lose by looking?
