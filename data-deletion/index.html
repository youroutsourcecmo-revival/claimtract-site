Delete your data

You can ask ClaimTract to delete the information we hold about you at any time. That includes anything we received when you connected a Facebook Page to ClaimTract.

To delete your data

  1. Email ezekiel@claimtract.app with the subject "Delete my data". Include the name and email on your ClaimTract account, or the Facebook Page you connected.
  2. We confirm the request by email and delete your data within 30 days.
  3. We email you when it's done.

Disconnect Facebook yourself

In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations (or Apps and websites). Find ClaimTract and select Remove. This stops ClaimTract from posting to your Page or reading anything from it. You can also disconnect your Page inside the ClaimTract Back office.

What gets deleted

Your Facebook access tokens, the Pages you connected, any post history we stored, and the account details tied to them. Records we must keep by law, such as payment records, are kept only as long as required.
