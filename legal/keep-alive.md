# CLAIMTRACT KEEP-ALIVE SERVICE

**Ezekiel Enterprises LLC — ClaimTract**

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

ClaimTract Keep-Alive is an optional, separate subscription available to eligible customers who discontinue active ClaimTract Pro services but wish to preserve their existing ClaimTract website.

Cancelling ClaimTract Pro never removes previously approved content. It stays published on ClaimTract's domain. Keep-Alive keeps that content connected to you.

While Keep-Alive is active, the existing content keeps your name and approval disclosure, its supported internal links, and supported contact pathways to your business telephone number or other designated contact destination, subject to technical availability.

Keep-Alive is a static service. It does not include new articles, new content generation, ongoing publishing, content research, content updates, or active optimization.

The customer receives a limited license to keep the existing content displayed with the customer's name and contact pathways for as long as the Keep-Alive subscription remains active and in good standing.

The customer does not acquire ownership of the website, articles, content clusters, internal linking architecture, or other ClaimTract-created materials.

Pricing and billing terms will be presented separately before enrollment.

If Keep-Alive is cancelled or payment obligations are not satisfied, the content remains published on ClaimTract's domain under ClaimTract's control, and ClaimTract may remove the customer's name, photograph, license number, contact details, and approval disclosure, present the content as general educational material, and change or remove its contact pathways, as described in Section 6.8 of the Terms of Use.
