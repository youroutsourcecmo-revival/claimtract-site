# CLAIMTRACT DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**Ezekiel Enterprises LLC**

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the ClaimTract Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

It applies when ClaimTract processes personal information submitted by a customer in connection with the customer's use of the service.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**Customer Data:** Information submitted, uploaded, recorded, or otherwise provided by a customer through ClaimTract.

**Personal Information:** Information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be associated with an identifiable individual, as defined by applicable law.

**Processing:** Any operation performed on personal information, including collection, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, modification, and deletion.

**Subprocessor:** A third-party service provider engaged by ClaimTract to process Customer Data in connection with providing the service.

## 2. ROLES OF THE PARTIES

The customer determines what information is submitted to ClaimTract and the purposes for which that information is submitted.

Where the customer submits personal information about homeowners, insureds, employees, or other individuals for processing through ClaimTract, the customer generally acts as the business, controller, or other responsible party under applicable privacy law, as determined by the circumstances.

Ezekiel Enterprises LLC processes that information to provide the requested services and generally acts as a service provider or processor where applicable law recognizes that role.

Ezekiel Enterprises LLC may separately act as a business or controller for its own account administration, billing, security, legal compliance, and other independent business purposes.

Nothing in this Addendum changes the legal status of either party where that status is determined by applicable law.

## 3. CUSTOMER INSTRUCTIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The customer represents and warrants that:

1. The customer has a lawful basis to submit the information.
2. The customer has obtained necessary permissions and authorizations.
3. The customer has provided required privacy notices to affected individuals.
4. The customer has authority to instruct ClaimTract to process the information.
5. The customer will not submit information in violation of applicable law.
6. The customer will not knowingly submit information that the customer is prohibited from disclosing.

The customer is responsible for determining whether any special legal restrictions apply to the information submitted.

The customer must not use ClaimTract as a substitute for a specialized secure records-management system unless a particular feature has expressly been designed and approved for that purpose.

## 4. CLAIMTRACT PROCESSING OBLIGATIONS

Ezekiel Enterprises LLC will:

1. Process Customer Data for providing, maintaining, and supporting ClaimTract.
2. Use Customer Data in accordance with the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and applicable law.
3. Use reasonable measures designed to protect Customer Data.
4. Restrict access to Customer Data to authorized personnel and service providers who need access for legitimate business purposes.
5. Use reasonable efforts to ensure that persons authorized to process Customer Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
6. Engage subprocessors as necessary to operate the service.
7. Provide reasonable assistance with applicable data protection obligations where required by law and appropriate to the service.

## 5. THIRD-PARTY SUBPROCESSORS

The customer authorizes ClaimTract to engage third-party providers to support the service.

Subprocessor categories may include:

- Cloud hosting and infrastructure.
- Database services.
- Artificial intelligence and content-generation services.
- Payment processing.
- Email and telephone communications.
- Text messaging.
- Security monitoring.
- Website hosting and delivery.
- Technical support.
- Analytics and diagnostics.

ClaimTract will use reasonable efforts to select providers appropriate for the functions they perform.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide information about relevant subprocessors and appropriate notice of material changes.

Subprocessors may process information in accordance with their applicable contractual arrangements.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party will protect confidential information received from the other party using reasonable safeguards.

ClaimTract will not intentionally disclose Customer Data to unrelated third parties except as authorized, necessary to provide the service, required by law, or otherwise permitted by the governing agreement and applicable law.

The customer is responsible for ensuring that information submitted to ClaimTract may lawfully be disclosed for processing.

## 7. SECURITY

ClaimTract will maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service and information processed.

No security system can guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure will never occur.

The customer is responsible for protecting account credentials and limiting access to authorized users.

## 8. SECURITY INCIDENTS

If ClaimTract becomes aware of a security incident involving Customer Data that requires notification under applicable law or this Addendum, we will investigate and provide notice as required.

Where reasonably practicable, information provided may include:

- The general nature of the incident.
- The categories of information affected.
- The known or estimated scope.
- Measures taken to investigate or address the incident.
- Additional information reasonably available.

The customer remains responsible for determining and fulfilling any separate notification obligations imposed on the customer by law.

Nothing in this section delays or overrides a statutory notification deadline.

## 9. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

If ClaimTract receives a privacy request concerning Customer Data for which the customer is the responsible business or controller, we may direct the individual to the customer.

Where legally required and technically feasible, we will provide reasonable assistance to the customer in responding to verified requests.

We may decline requests that are unlawful, technically infeasible, outside the scope of the service, or inconsistent with applicable legal obligations.

## 10. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Customer Data will be retained according to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Upon account termination or a valid deletion request, ClaimTract will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify applicable Customer Data, subject to:

- Legal retention requirements.
- Accounting and financial record obligations.
- Security and fraud-prevention requirements.
- Pending disputes or legal proceedings.
- Backup and disaster-recovery limitations.
- Technical limitations.
- Other lawful retention purposes.

Data retained under these exceptions will remain subject to applicable confidentiality and security requirements.

## 11. INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Customer Data may be processed in countries where ClaimTract or its subprocessors operate.

Where required by law, appropriate transfer mechanisms and safeguards will be used.

## 12. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE INFORMATION

Upon reasonable written request, ClaimTract may provide information reasonably available to demonstrate its compliance with applicable obligations under this Addendum.

Any audit or assessment must be reasonable in scope, protect other customers' information, avoid unnecessary disruption, and preserve the confidentiality and security of ClaimTract's systems.

Nothing in this section requires disclosure of source code, proprietary architecture, confidential security details, or information relating to other customers.

## 13. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

If this Addendum conflicts with the Terms of Use regarding the processing of Customer Data, this Addendum controls to the extent required to resolve that conflict.

All other provisions of the Terms of Use remain in effect.
