Privacy policy

ClaimTract, a service of Ezekiel Enterprises LLC. DRAFT, pending legal review. Last updated November 21, 2026.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers two groups: public adjusters who use ClaimTract (the free website, the field app and the paid service), and homeowners and visitors who open an adjuster's page.

2. What we collect from adjusters

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We do not see your Google password.
  • Your profile: what you enter, such as phone, city, service area, hours, story, memberships and logo.
  • License check: the Florida DFS licensee page you give us, and the public license details it lists. We recheck it weekly.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it: permission to create events with a Google Meet link on your own calendar, for bookings and instant video calls. We create those events only when you or a homeowner book a call or you tap "Instant video call". We do not read your other calendar events, and Google is told not to email any guest.
  • Recordings: when you record a section with the mic, the audio is transcribed on our own server and the text is used to rewrite that section in your words.
  • Use of the app: events such as sign-in, version picked, pages shared and buttons tapped, so we can run and improve the service.

3. What we collect from homeowners and visitors

  • Visits: that a page was opened, read for a minute, saved as a contact or reached by QR code, with a random visitor number stored on your device. We do not store your IP address with these events.
  • Claim binder: your call log, damage list, checklist, questions and photos of letters stay on your phone. They are sent to the adjuster only after you tick the consent box and tap Share. After that, new entries are shared automatically until you turn sharing off.
  • Bookings and messages: what you enter to book a time or reach the adjuster, such as your name, phone, email and address.

4. How we use it

  • To run your page and app, show alerts to the adjuster, and keep accounts secure.
  • To help the adjuster work the claim you shared with him.
  • With your consent, and only after your name, address and anything else that identifies you are removed, the adjuster may use what happened on your claim in his articles and ads to help other homeowners.
  • We do not sell personal information.

5. Who we share it with

  • The adjuster whose page you used, for what you chose to share with him.
  • Service providers that run the service for us, such as hosting, Google (sign-in and Calendar), Stripe (payments) and an AI writing service that rewrites an adjuster's own sections. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • When the law requires it.

6. Google user data

ClaimTract's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to sign you in and to create the Meet events you ask for. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

7. Texts and calls

ClaimTract does not text or call homeowners on an adjuster's behalf. When an adjuster sends a link or a video call invitation, it goes from his own phone. Any texts from ClaimTract to adjusters are covered by the separate agreement accepted on the sign-up form, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

8. How long we keep it

We keep adjuster accounts while they are active and delete them on request. Shared binders stay with the adjuster's account. Nightly backups are kept for 7 days.

9. Your choices

  • Adjusters can edit their profile, disconnect Google Calendar in their Google account settings, or ask us to delete their account.
  • Homeowners can keep their binder on their phone only, turn sharing off at any time, or ask us to delete anything they shared.

10. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Sessions are stored as one-way hashes, and keys are kept off the public site. No system is perfectly secure, so we tell affected users if we learn of a breach that affects their information.

11. Children

ClaimTract is for adults handling property claims. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change affects your rights in a significant way, we will tell you before it applies to you.
