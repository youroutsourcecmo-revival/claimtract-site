HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. That's the whole job.

Upload one claim at a timeEvery photo from that one claim or job, and nothing from any other. Sophia drops the blurry ones and the near-duplicates, checks the rest against building code and Florida statutes, and keeps her best picks. Changed your mind? Bring back any photo she dropped.
Talk to the homeownerShe shows you her picks one at a time. Tap the mic once and describe each photo like you're standing in their kitchen. Swipe right to keep it. If you'd say the same thing you said about the last photo, swipe it away.
Forward that claim's emailsThe carrier emails are the timeline: when the claim was filed, what the carrier said and how long they took. That's the story homeowners need to hear, and photos can't tell it. Add the dec page if you have it, so Sophia knows what the policy covers. Names and policy numbers get scrubbed.
